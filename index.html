Recent research.

Selected projects across real-time brain decoding, foundation models, benchmarks, EEG, pathology, and efficient reasoning.

fMRI · Brain decoding · 2026 preprint

Realtime MindEye reconstructs visual perception from fMRI within seconds.

Built with RT-Cloud and a real-time-compatible MindEye2 pipeline, this proof of concept reconstructs viewed images from single-trial 3T fMRI while a participant is still in the scanner.

Read the preprint ↗ View the project ↗
fMRI foundation models · ICML 2026

CortexMAE + Brainmarks

CortexMAE scales masked autoencoding over flat-map videos of open fMRI data. Brainmarks tests what those representations transfer to across more than 30 benchmark tasks.

Release post Paper Models
Medical LLM evaluation · 2026

Medmarks

A fully open medical LLM evaluation suite spanning 30 benchmarks and both verifiable and open-ended clinical tasks, with results for 61 models across 71 configurations.

Live leaderboard Release post Paper
EEG-to-image · 2026 preprint

ENIGMA

A compact multi-subject EEG-to-image model that adapts to a new person with as little as 15 minutes of data and uses less than 1% of the trainable parameters of prior approaches.

Read the preprint
Pathology foundation models · 2025 report

OpenMidnight

An open pathology foundation model trained end to end on 12,000 public whole-slide images for about $1,600, with the code, training pipeline, and weights released.

Technical report Citable archive
